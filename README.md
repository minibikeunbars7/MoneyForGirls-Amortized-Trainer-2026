## 🎮 About the Game

Money for Girls: Amortized is a horror-romance visual novel and RPG developed by Porpentine and published by INNOCENT Interactive. It was released on August 24, 2026, for Windows, macOS, and Linux. The game follows a young girl detective trying to help her mother escape crippling debt while navigating school, romance, fashion, investigation, and disturbing bad endings.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Money for Girls: Amortized**. ## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Money for Girls: Amortized does not use conventional free-roaming movement. Progression takes place through visual-novel scenes, location selections, dialogue, and branching choices. Use the trainer after loading a save and before making important route decisions.

### Tools & Inventory

The game's collectible systems include outfits, stickers, photographs, artwork, and music. Unlock functions affect these persistent collections, while cash supports purchases and financial decisions during the current playthrough.

### World & Environment

Explore the locations of Sororal Quasisland through nonlinear and optional scenes. Resource controls can make it easier to maintain mood and hygiene while spending more time investigating, pursuing relationships, and collecting gallery content.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no standard combat system. Conflict is handled through dialogue, investigation, financial pressure, relationships, and branching decisions. The trainer therefore focuses on resources, route experimentation, and collectible progression rather than combat statistics.
